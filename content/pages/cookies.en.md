**This site does not sell your privacy.** We use no tracking cookies and no third-party advertising, so there is no intrusive banner.

### What we use
- **Technical session cookie** (`gs`): set only if you sign in to the partner area. It keeps you signed in, expires after a few days and is strictly necessary for the service.
- **Theme preference** (light/dark) and language: saved in your browser (`localStorage`) and never sent to anyone.
- **Anonymous statistics** (only if enabled by the owner): we use a privacy-friendly tool that sets no cookies and does not track individual visitors.

### Payments
When you choose to pay by card you are taken to Stripe's secure page, which applies its own policy.
