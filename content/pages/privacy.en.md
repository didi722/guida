### Data we collect
- **Bookings:** name, email, phone (optional), number of guests, notes, any invoicing details.
- **Partner (B2B) accounts:** company and contact details (company name, VAT number, SDI/PEC code, name, email, phone) and password (stored only in encrypted form).
- **Messages** sent through the contact form.
- **Minimal technical data** in server logs (IP address, date and time of requests) for security and abuse prevention.

### Why and on what basis
- To carry out the booking you requested and communicate with you (performance of a contract).
- To meet tax and accounting obligations (legal obligation).
- To keep the site secure and prevent abuse (legitimate interest).
- To answer your messages (pre-contractual measures / legitimate interest).

We do not profile users and we do not sell data to third parties.

### Who we share it with
Only with providers needed to run the service, acting as processors: the card payment provider (Stripe), the email service and the hosting provider. Data may be disclosed to tax advisers and to authorities where required by law.

### How long we keep it
Booking data is kept for the period required by tax and accounting rules (normally 10 years). Contact messages are kept as long as needed to reply. Partner accounts are kept until closed.

### Your rights
You can ask for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection by writing to [{{email}}](mailto:{{email}}). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante ([garanteprivacy.it](https://www.garanteprivacy.it)), or with your local authority.
