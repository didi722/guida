## Hi, I'm Davide.

I am a **licensed tour guide in Venice** and a **historian of religions**. Before walking people through the alleys I spent years among books, libraries and journeys (mostly in Japan) studying how human beings believe, ritualise and tell the sacred.

I bring that outlook to Venice too. For me it is not an open-air museum but a city built by people who had a faith, a market, fears and ambitions: merchants, exiles, ambassadors, craftspeople, different communities that learned to live together (not always well).

### How I work

- **Stories, not lists.** Fewer dates, more questions: why is it like this? Who decided? What did it mean to the people walking past it every day?
- **Small groups.** You walk better, listen better and can ask a few more questions.
- **Tailor-made.** If you are short on time, travelling with children or have a specific interest, we build the route together.
- **Professional for the travel trade.** If you are a DMC, tour operator or agency, there is [a dedicated section](/en/b2b) with reserved rates, invoicing and clear terms.
